The Accursed Lexus Project

Welcome to the official build tracker for my 1995 Lexus SC400 that I got for... $950. Getting this car to run smoothly has been a test of patience, requiring a mix of mechanical troubleshooting and custom electrical fabrication.

Currently, the car does not run. It needs a new starter, and some more parts, and the interior electronics are coming together. There is still a lot of work to do before it is a reliable daily driver.

Recent Milestones

  • Got it started as it was.
  • Found out it had no power steering, added power steering fluid to steer easier. Discovered the problem that was the reason why the previous owner wanted to sell the car.
  • Fixed the problem (it was a mismatched Idle Air Control valve!). First test drive.
  • Car does not start, even after a jump. Could it be a broken starter? (On cars with this engine, the starter is an at least 7-hour job)
  • Extracted the starter. It was really broken!
