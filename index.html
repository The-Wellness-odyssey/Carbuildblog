The Accursed Lexus Project

Welcome to the official build tracker for my early 2000s Lexus. Getting this car to run smoothly has been a test of patience, requiring a mix of mechanical troubleshooting and custom electrical fabrication.

Currently, the car is finally sitting right on its new suspension, and the interior electronics are coming together. There is still a lot of work to do before it is a reliable daily driver, but the major hurdles are behind me.
